# Presenting the work honestly

Walk through the business problem, requirements, alternatives, design decisions, implementation, migration risks, recovery evidence and independent handover. Explain why improving the incumbent might beat replacement. Demonstrate one fictional runbook and one recovery decision instead of reading the directory tree aloud.

Safe initial description: “Created a vendor-neutral documentation-platform blueprint covering requirements, evaluation, container deployment, migration, security, operations and handover, with a Docmost reference configuration.”

After real delivery, add only verified outcomes with employer authorization: migrated scope, adoption measures, recovery time and acceptance evidence. Keep customer content and infrastructure private. Public examples should be newly created synthetic material, not lightly redacted exports that retain names, URLs or hidden metadata.

This repository is a reference artifact. It does not by itself prove a production rollout, uptime result, successful restore or business impact. Add anonymized evidence only after it exists and is approved for publication.
