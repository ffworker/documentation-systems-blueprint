# Administrator handover

Give the receiving operator the private architecture/configuration inventory, approved versions/digests, access group, vault references, escalation contacts and maintenance calendar. Transfer credentials through the approved vault, never in a document attachment. Verify access independently before revoking temporary delivery accounts.

Conduct a supervised walkthrough of deployment, logs, health checks, backup encryption/transfer, restore, permission management and updates. Then have the receiver restore a sample in isolation and diagnose a simulated dependency failure without coaching. Record elapsed time, problems and follow-up actions.

Confirm that recovery instructions and backup keys remain accessible during an identity or documentation outage. Agree support boundaries, warranty/transition window, open defects, capacity ownership and change approval. Final handover requires receiver and service-owner acceptance, not merely sending a link.
