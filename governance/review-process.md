# Review process

The author submits a draft with owner, change reason, affected service and evidence. A domain reviewer checks accuracy and follows the procedure where feasible. An operator reviews disruptive steps and recovery; a security reviewer checks sensitive access changes. The owner publishes or records a reasoned rejection.

Review checklist: intended audience clear; prerequisites complete; commands scoped; success criteria observable; rollback/data-loss consequences stated; links valid; no secrets; classifications and access correct; dependencies and review date current.

If the selected platform lacks enforced approval workflow, use a restricted draft space and a private review register. The steward moves approved content into the published structure. Record reviewer, version and date so procedural approval is auditable.

For urgent incident corrections, publish the minimum verified fix under an accountable incident owner and schedule retrospective review within two working days. Track overdue reviews weekly; escalate critical overdue procedures to the service owner rather than silently renewing dates.
