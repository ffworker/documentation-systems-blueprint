# Ownership and accountability

| Activity | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope, funding, service objectives | Service owner | Delivery lead | Users, security, operators |
| Infrastructure and recovery | Service owner | Platform operator | Security, content owners |
| Content accuracy | Domain/content owner | Authors | Operational users |
| Taxonomy and review cadence | Knowledge steward | Content owners | Authors |
| Access approval | Domain/content owner | Access administrator | Security |
| Migration acceptance | Service owner | Migration lead | Content owners, operators |
| Retirement and retention | Service owner | Platform operator | Records/privacy stakeholders |

Map roles to actual people and deputies in a private register. One person may hold several roles, but critical recovery/access approval should have independent verification. Ownership includes accepting stale-content risk, not only editing pages.

Track a small set of useful measures: critical pages with owners, overdue reviews, time to find benchmark answers, successful onboarding tasks, restore success and unresolved migration exceptions. Measure a baseline and later results; do not invent percentage improvements for a portfolio.
